## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Create a Project:** Click "New Project" on the dashboard
2. **Add Devices:** Choose from Laptop, Phone, Tablet, Browser, Monitor
3. **Upload Screenshots:** Drag & drop or click to upload
4. **Choose Background:** 
   - Procedural backgrounds (gradients, patterns)
   - Image backgrounds (60+ high-res options)
   - Hybrid (image + vector effects)
5. **Add Icons:** Search from 100+ icons, customize and place
6. **Customize:** Adjust text, logo, decorations, lighting
7. **Surprise Me:** Generate random professional compositions
8. **Export:** Download as PNG/JPG/WebP at any resolution
